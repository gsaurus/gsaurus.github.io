Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers the Streets of Rage 2 New Era game, its editors and tools, and this website (www.sor2newera.com). New Era is a free fan project. It does not sell data, show personalised ads or ask for accounts.

What the game sends online

  • Online leaderboards and challenges: when you submit a result, the game sends the player name you chose, your score or time, the game settings, a replay of your inputs and a random install identifier used to prevent duplicate submissions. Player names, results and winning replays are shown publicly in the game and may appear in videos of winning runs.
  • Online play: to connect players, the game exchanges network addresses and game inputs with the other players in the session.
  • Twitch integration (optional): if you link a Twitch account, the game uses it only to read and post chat messages for the Twitch game mode. You can unlink it at any time.

Everything else, such as settings, local scores and replays, stays on your device.

Website

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits. Google may set cookies for this; see Google's privacy policy.

YouTube videos

A tool run by the developer records winning Weekly Challenge replays and uploads them to the project's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API. It does not access other users' YouTube data. See Google's privacy policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Removal and contact

To have your leaderboard entries or a video of your run removed, or for any privacy question, contact the developer through the GitHub profile.
